Privacy Policy — SkinLens

Effective September 14, 2026

SkinLens is a skincare routine and ingredient-checking app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate — if anything is unclear, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

The short version

  • You can use SkinLens fully as a guest — your routine, skin profile, and saved scans are stored only on your device, never on our servers, never tied to an identity.
  • If you choose to sign in, we collect your email address to send you a one-time login code and keep you signed in across app restarts.
  • When you check an ingredient list, the text you enter is sent to our server to look up each ingredient and return a result — it isn't linked to who you are unless you're signed in.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs, and we don't sell your data.

Information stored only on your device

By default, SkinLens works without an account ("guest mode"). In this mode, the following is stored locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage, and is never transmitted to us as part of an account or profile:

  • Your skin type and skin concerns, if you choose to enter them
  • Your routine (product names, morning/night assignment, frequency, and completion history)
  • Your reminder times, tone preference, and mute setting
  • Ingredient scans you choose to save, and their results

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it for you.

Information we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional and lets your routine sync across devices. If you sign in with your email, we collect:

  • Your email address — used to send a one-time 6-digit login code, and to identify your account going forward.
  • A signed session token — stored securely on your device (using your platform's secure credential storage, e.g. Keychain on iOS) so you don't have to re-enter a code every time you open the app.
  • The same routine, skin profile, and scan data described above, but associated with your account so it can sync across your devices.

We do not require or collect your name, a password, or any other personal identifier to create an account.

Ingredient analysis

When you paste or type an ingredient list to check, that text is sent to our server, which looks up each ingredient against our reference data and returns a plain-language result. For guests, this request is not linked to any identity. If you're signed in, a record of scans you choose to save is stored against your account so you can find them again later.

What we don't do

  • We don't run advertising, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We don't require access to your camera roll, contacts, or location to use the app.

Data retention and deletion

Guest data lives entirely on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage. If you have a signed-in account and want your account and associated data deleted from our servers, contact us at the email below and we'll process the request.

Children's privacy

SkinLens is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is handled, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
